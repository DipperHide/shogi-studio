Modified for this app: removed the illustrated wooden body and edge inscription;
retained the original face outlines, fitted them to the original 3D pieces, and
composited them with reference-guided generated wood textures. Derived glyph SVGs, masks and
piece-face atlases are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.
Source and derivative files: assets/calligraphy/ryoko and assets/textures/pieces.jpg.
The full license is included as CC-BY-SA-4.0-Ryoko.txt.

Yuji Syuku remains used for board coordinates under SIL OFL 1.1.
Noto Sans SC is used for the interface under SIL OFL 1.1.
Noto Sans JP and Noto Serif JP are bundled under SIL OFL 1.1 for Japanese
interface text and selectable Mincho piece lettering. See the adjacent OFL files.
Sources: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansjp
         https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoserifjp

Version 0.6.0 adds white-tinted, cropped Ryoko SVG outlines for the minimal
renderer. Original outlines remain unchanged; viewBox and fill were adjusted
for legibility in both themes. These derivatives remain CC BY-SA 4.0.
Transformation and hashes: scripts/prepare_minimal_glyphs.py and
godot/assets/glyphs/provenance.json.

Wood textures in classic 0.4.2 were generated using image_gen from user-provided
photographic references, then color-calibrated and cropped for each wooden part.
Source assets and prompts: assets/materials/photographic-v042.

Classic 0.4.3 adds a dedicated fine tsuge wood texture, lossless 4096 x 4096
piece-face and ink atlases, and finer rounded piece bevels.
Texture and generation prompt: assets/materials/piece-v043.
